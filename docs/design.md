# Design specification

Original visual direction: warm paper #f7f6f2, ink #1b2420, forest #214e42, brass accent #875322, quiet gray green surfaces. Manrope variable font, generously spaced typography, 12/16/20/24/30/36/48/72 px hierarchy. Buttons at least 44 px, restrained 12–16 px radii, no blurred cover imagery or decorative glass panels.

The generated hero reference established a left aligned two column composition, large curiosity headline, physical brass and green twin pendulum, one primary action and an unboxed prediction strip. The working frontend translates that reference into original SVG and actual controls. Generated pictures are not proof of functionality.

Reviewed Mobbin [Brilliant lesson screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/0806e864-e953-46f7-8410-7ea00e69aac7): one visible experiment, a short instruction and a clear check action. Use that hierarchy, not its protected illustrations. The Ahead reference emphasizes research provenance; Trialroom instead links its actual equations and limitations and does not borrow its university badges. The [untitled] music reference was reviewed before research rejected the music idea; no music controls are shipped.

Four signature components: physical twin pendulum; A/B/Same prediction strip; measured outcome panel; compact mobile Home/Studio/Guide bar. The two meaningful motion cues are actual instrument movement from model time and a prediction window countdown. Reduced motion keeps the final readable state; no autoplaying decorative physics on Home.

Anatomy informed physical construction, tone hierarchy, depth, bearings, calibration ticks and motion from one model. The SVG implementation is original, not a kit geometry audit; no Anatomy audit or native 3D certification is claimed. Each experiment has a different physical scene; none is a static product photograph.

Native image generation supplied the design reference and requested marketing thumbnail.
